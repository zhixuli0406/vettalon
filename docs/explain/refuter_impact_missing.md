# refuter_impact_missing: The refuter said "plausible" but gave no usable impact rating

## What it means

A refuter run that judged the claim plausible was asked to rate the impact (low to critical) independently. It left that out or gave an unusable value, so there is nothing to compare with the finder's rating. The finding's severity does not change and the verdict stands.

## Why you see it

The note reads `refuter_impact_missing: run i/n`, naming the run.

## What to do

Judge the impact yourself before relying on the severity: what an attacker gains if the flaw is real.
