# operator_review: A human reviewer recorded a decision on this finding

## What it means

This evidence item records a person's decision: confirmed, refuted or suppressed. A scan never writes it. A model verdict cannot stand in for it, and the latest operator decision takes precedence over model evidence.

## Why you see it

A reviewer used a triage tool, such as the dashboard, on the report. The tool added this item and set the finding's severity basis to `operator`.

## What to do

Read the decision and its reason before you plan the fix. If you disagree, record a new decision. The newest one counts.
