# no_maintainer_confirmation: No person has confirmed this finding

## What it means

The finding's evidence level is below `maintainer_confirmed`. Agreement between models and PoC results do not stand in for a human decision. Vettalon never sets `confirmed` on its own.

## Why you see it

No reviewer has marked the finding confirmed. This gap appears on nearly every finding straight from a scan.

## What to do

Have a responsible person read the evidence and record a decision (confirmed, refuted or suppressed) in a review tool such as the dashboard. `needs_human` findings are the ones waiting for this.
